Anima Generator + Anima Preview

playground mockup · docs/generator-design.md
Behavioural reference for the two nodes in AnimaFlow/Anima. Everything here is clickable — toggle stages and watch the three image outputs re-resolve, flip use_internal_loaders and watch the resource sockets go dead, add detailer blocks and watch their SEGS sockets appear, hover the compare to wipe. Same palette as playground/theme.html; nothing here fetches anything.
Mockup, not the node. The images are procedural SVGs standing in for real stage output — they differ in resolution on purpose (1024 → 2048), because that mismatch is the thing the wipe has to survive. Litegraph resize, socket typing, and the settings-blob round trip are all out of scope here.

Open questions this mockup exists to settle
Open · one tabbed overlay, or five dialogs Upstream ships one dialog per stage — five files, ~130k of JS (web/js/aio/*_settings_dialog.js). This mockup implements the single tabbed overlay alternative. Click any ⚙ to judge it. The tab strip carries a dot per enabled stage, so "what's actually on" is readable without opening anything.
Open · MAX_DETAILER_PASSES Upstream is N addable blocks, not two — face/eye built in, custom_N beyond them, all renameable and reorderable, effectively uncapped. The Detailer tab mirrors that, with each block owning a SEGS socket in place of upstream's per-block detect_prompt. Capped at 4 here because every pass is a full re-sample — the number is the open question, and it can grow later, never shrink.
Settled · pass-through on a disabled stage A disabled stage's output carries the previous stage's image. The resolver above spells out which, and flags in amber when two outputs are carrying the same tensor — so image_mid == image_base reads as a documented state, not a bug.
